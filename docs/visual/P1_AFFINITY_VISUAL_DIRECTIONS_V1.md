# Daily Town P1 — Affinity Visual Directions v1

Status: candidate directions only. Image production requires human selection first.

## Goal

Prepare three visual directions for `familiar / trusted / best_friend` that stay inside the already-approved safe affinity matrix and preserve Moru A-2 canonical anatomy.

Shared constraints:

- anatomy, face geometry and primary silhouette remain locked
- progression communicates shared history, not power escalation
- map-avatar may simplify or omit affinity detail for readability
- no affinity stage may become a gameplay requirement for state readability
- animated combinations must avoid expression × lighting × affinity × motion asset explosion

## Direction AF-1 — Memory Keepsakes

Core idea: every affinity stage adds a small object tied to shared exploration memories.

Progression:

- `familiar`: small scarf stitch/badge or first-walk tag
- `trusted`: bag charm + scarf detail coordinated around one remembered place/theme
- `best_friend`: a restrained set of keepsakes, still using the canonical scarf/bag silhouette

Visual feeling:

- personal
- sentimental
- story-driven
- strongest fit with Journal/Memory

Advantages:

- makes affinity feel earned through experiences rather than level-up power
- integrates naturally with A-3 memory records
- easy to keep modular and replaceable
- low silhouette risk

Risks:

- tiny keepsakes can disappear at map-avatar size
- requires good metadata/story linkage to feel meaningful rather than decorative

Daily Town fit: **very high**.

## Direction AF-2 — Botanical Growth

Core idea: Moru's botanical motifs become slightly richer as the relationship grows.

Progression:

- `familiar`: small sprout accent or subtle leaf stitch
- `trusted`: coordinated leaf/flower accent + cloth detail
- `best_friend`: fuller but still compact sprout decoration, plus small botanical bag/scarf motif

Visual feeling:

- organic
- cozy
- highly brand-consistent

Advantages:

- instantly compatible with Soft Botanical Explorer
- strong visual continuity across characters and map effects
- does not require large props

Risks:

- can blur the line between affinity and biological evolution
- larger sprout changes may alter canonical silhouette
- future non-botanical companions need equivalent but different motif logic

Daily Town fit: **high**, with silhouette ceiling caution.

## Direction AF-3 — Explorer Patina

Core idea: affinity appears as evidence of many walks together—patched cloth, travel tag, worn-in bag, route token.

Progression:

- `familiar`: subtle cloth trim/route pin
- `trusted`: patched bag or travel tag + coordinated scarf detail
- `best_friend`: curated explorer set with one signature memory accessory

Visual feeling:

- lived-in
- adventurous
- less ornamental than AF-2

Advantages:

- reinforces walking/exploration as the core behavior
- works for nearly any future companion family
- high semantic scalability across locations and seasons

Risks:

- too many patches can become visually noisy
- may read as equipment progression if overdone

Daily Town fit: **high**.

## Comparison

| Criterion | AF-1 Memory Keepsakes | AF-2 Botanical Growth | AF-3 Explorer Patina |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Emotional memory link | Excellent | Good | Good |
| Brand botanical fit | Good | Excellent | Good |
| Future companion scalability | Excellent | Medium | Excellent |
| 48 dp silhouette safety | Excellent | Medium | Good |
| Risk of power-up reading | Low | Medium | Medium |
| A-3 integration | Excellent | Good | Good |

## Recommended production direction

Recommended default: **AF-1 Memory Keepsakes**, with small AF-3 explorer-wear cues as a secondary vocabulary.

Reason:

Daily Town's core loop ends in `record / collection / memory`, so affinity feels more distinctive when the character visibly carries traces of shared discoveries rather than simply becoming more ornate.
